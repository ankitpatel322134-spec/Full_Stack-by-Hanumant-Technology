Grandma's Secret Apple Pie

This Apple Pie Recipe Has Been In My Family For Generations And Necer Fails To Delight.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup suger
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 6 medium-sized apples, peeled and sliced

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375oF (190 oC)
  2. In a bowl, mix the sugar and cinnamon .
  3. Toss the apples in the sugar-cinnamon mixture.
